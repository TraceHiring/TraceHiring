Before you start: exactly what is recorded

[Employer name] uses Trace to check that answers are your own work. You will see the same report the employer sees, and you can explain or appeal anything it shows. A person makes every decision.

We record

  • Typing timing in answer boxes only (not which keys you press elsewhere)
  • Pastes, and when you leave or return to the test tab
  • Whether one face is visible, and how long you look away
  • Your spoken answers, only in voice questions
  • A few still images, background blurred, only around a moment a person needs to check

We never

  • Guess your emotions, stress or personality
  • Store a face or voice template to identify you
  • Record your full session on video
  • Reject you automatically
  • Use your data to train models without permission

Who sees it, and for how long

The hiring team and you. Clips are deleted after 90 days (longer only while an appeal is open). You can ask for your data or its deletion at any time. Full notice · Grievance contact

If you use a screen reader, dictation, a switch device or need extra time, tell us privately. The employer only sees that an accommodation was applied, never the reason.
