## Security, privacy and reliability (what was checked clean)
- **Service-role and secret keys (S3).** `SUPABASE_SECRET_KEY_JOBS` appears only in `workers/jobs`. Nothing service-role is in `apps/**`, `workers/edge/**` or `services/api/**` outside tests and redaction regexes, and `scan-dist` is clean on the fresh build.
- **Candidate token and IDOR at the edge (S4).** Every session route, the media route included, goes through `sessionAuth` (`routes-session.ts:76-83`). Positions, section ids and media hashes are re-checked against the session's own draw (`session-core.ts:884-975`).
- **Server-side roles (S5).** Every M1 route calls `requireRole`. Locks and invites need an admin at aal2, and locks are enforced again in the DB on every save, publish and from-template.
- **Input validation (S6).** Bodies go through strict zod and a capped stream reader that refuses `__proto__`. Image uploads are capped at 1 MiB with magic bytes checked. The item-variants evaluator is bounded. The media rate limit is 240/min. Candidate CSP is unchanged; the recruiter adds only `worker-src 'self'`.
- **LLM drafter (S8).** No identity goes in the request, and the topic is scrubbed twice. Output is validated by strict zod. Drafts land `in_review` only. The mock refuses to start outside dev/staging, and provider, model and prompt are pinned.
- **Errors (S11).** Errors are constant codes and never carry a message or details.
- **Audit (S12).** Audit details are ids, enums and counts only. Every transition is audited by DB guards, and M1 adds no `audit_log` grant.
- **Local answer buffer (S14).** Answers stay in memory and are stripped before anything goes to sessionStorage.
- **No PII (P1).** No `console.*` in either app. Logs carry ids, codes and SQLSTATEs only. Invites store an HMAC with a pepper of at least 32 bytes. Import errors carry no cell value.
- **Database (S1, C8).** All 19 M1 tables have RLS with per-operation policies and WITH CHECK, no `using (true)`, and predicate indexes. DEFINER functions use an empty `search_path` and EXECUTE is revoked. The three views are `security_invoker`. Migrations are forward-only. D1 pgTAP edits are limited to the R5-28/R5-50 list.
- **Reliability (C1, C2, C5–C7).**
  - The draw is stored in the DO before the first serve, section clocks run on the server, and answers at unserved positions are refused.
  - Mirror v2 is insert-once, so there is one exposure event. Quality v2 re-checks the layout and `package_version`. Imports and drafting are deterministic and locked.
  - Versions are recorded (`band-engine-2`, `m1-scoring-v1`, `package_version`, template and prompt versions).
  - Timeouts are 60 s on the drafter and 10 s on Auth admin calls. Each queue has its own DLQ, and vendors sit behind interfaces with mocks.
- **Test health (H2, H3).**
  - No test was weakened. Deleted `authoring.test.ts` cases moved to `draft-view.test.ts:112-139`. The `tests.test.ts` write list follows the move to the atomic RPC.
  - Floors went up: rls 2633 → 3893, catalog 144 → 150.
  - No TODO, FIXME or HACK in M1 code.
- **ADRs (H5).** The only new runtime dependency is `fflate` 0.8.3 (ADR-033). The zod bump is logged, and the two new packages are internal.
